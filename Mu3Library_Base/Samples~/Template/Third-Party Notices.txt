This sample ships third-party content so that its TextMesh Pro text renders without the TMP Essential Resources.

Fonts/Sample Sans SDF.asset
  The TextMesh Pro font asset that TextMesh Pro built from Liberation Sans (LiberationSans SDF in the TMP
  Essential Resources), with its material set to Shaders/TMP_SDF-Mobile.shader and its fallback font removed.
  It is a Modified Version of that font under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1, and is distributed under
  the same license: Fonts/LiberationSans - OFL.txt. The license reserves the name "Liberation" for the original
  font, so the asset is named "Sample Sans".

Shaders/TMP_SDF-Mobile.shader, Shaders/TMPro_Properties.cginc
  The "TextMeshPro/Mobile/Distance Field" shader and its property include from the TMP Essential Resources of
  com.unity.ugui 2.6.0, with the shader renamed so that it does not clash with those resources.
  uGUI copyright (c) 2015 Unity Technologies.
  Licensed under the Unity Companion License for Unity-dependent projects:
  https://unity.com/legal/licenses/unity-companion-license

Fonts/NotoSansKR-Bold.ttf, Fonts/NotoSansKR-Bold SDF.asset
  Noto Sans KR Bold and the TextMesh Pro font asset built from it. The font is cut down with fontTools
  pyftsubset to printable ASCII, the characters of KS X 1001 other than its Hanja (the 2,350 precomposed
  Hangul syllables, the Hangul compatibility jamo, kana, and the symbols), and U+65E5 U+672C U+8A9E, the native
  name of the sample's Japanese locale. Other characters, such as the Hangul syllables outside KS X 1001 and
  most Hanja and kanji, do not render with it. The subset is a Modified Version of the font under the SIL Open
  Font License, Version 1.1, and keeps the font's names, none of which is the Reserved Font Name.

  Licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1: Fonts/NotoSansKR-Bold - OFL.txt
